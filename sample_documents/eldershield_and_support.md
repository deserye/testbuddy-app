# ElderShield and Support — Learning Summary

ElderShield is a closed historical long-term-care insurance scheme. Benefits and plan details depend on the historical plan joined, so users should check their actual coverage through CPF Board member services rather than infer it from a general explanation.

AIC provides public guidance on CareShield Life applications, documents, eFASS, accredited severe-disability assessors, and support for caregivers. MOH publishes information about CareShield Life and ElderShield supplements; supplement benefits differ by product and should be verified through official comparison materials and the relevant insurer.

This sample document is for demonstrating document upload and retrieval only. It does not assess disability, decide eligibility, interpret medical evidence, or recommend insurance products.

Official sources:
- https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/healthcare-financing/eldershield
- https://www.aic.sg/Financial-Assistance/CareShield-Life
- https://www.moh.gov.sg/managing-expenses/schemes-and-subsidies/careshield-life/careshield-life-and-eldershield-supplements/

Key retrieval topics: ElderShield, historical coverage, AIC, applications, eFASS, assessors, caregivers, supplements, official support.
