# CareCompass SG research notes

## Official sources

- [CPF Board — CareShield Life](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/healthcare-financing/careshield-life): long-term-care insurance, joining context, benefits, premiums, subsidies, claims, six activities of daily living, and official services.
- [CPF Board — ElderShield](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/healthcare-financing/eldershield): closed historical scheme, plan-dependent benefits, coverage-check route, premiums, and claims.
- [AIC — CareShield Life](https://www.aic.sg/Financial-Assistance/CareShield-Life): qualification guidance, application steps, documents, eFASS, MOH-accredited assessors, and support for applications on behalf of someone who lacks mental capacity.
- [MOH — CareShield Life and ElderShield Supplements](https://www.moh.gov.sg/managing-expenses/schemes-and-subsidies/careshield-life/careshield-life-and-eldershield-supplements/): supplements, payment context, coverage checks, and comparison materials.

## Product decision

The application focuses on two public-information journeys rather than a personal calculator. The first explains the high-level distinction between CareShield Life, ElderShield, and supplements, then directs users to CPF Board secure member services for actual coverage. The second produces a generic preparation checklist for a care or claim conversation and points users to CPF Board, AIC, MOH, or a healthcare provider.

## Safety decision

The application must not diagnose disability, interpret a medical assessment, predict a claim decision, calculate a personal payout, recommend private insurance, rank insurers, or request medical records, NRIC details, account numbers, exact balances, addresses, or other identifying data. All current and personal information must be verified through official services.
